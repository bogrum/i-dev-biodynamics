I study how proteins open binding pockets as they move, and how to find those pockets in molecular dynamics simulations before they show up in any crystal structure.

Research

PocketHunter

Lead developer at Gebze Technical University, with Dr. Onur Serçinoğlu, since 2023

PocketHunter extracts frames from MD trajectories, predicts pockets in each frame with P2Rank and clusters them, so that persistent, transient and cryptic sites can be tracked and compared. PocketHunter Suite is the public web platform around it, with SMINA docking and a Mol* viewer. A manuscript is in preparation for the Nucleic Acids Research Web Server Issue. Code, web platform code.

Structural modelling of peptide–MHC complexes

Scholarship holder at the Health Institutes of Türkiye (TÜSEB), Turkish Cancer Institute, since 2025

In a national project on neoantigen discovery for mRNA cancer vaccines, I model peptide–MHC complexes with deep-learning structure predictors (Chai-1, Boltz-2) and assess them with replicated MD simulations. My MSc thesis builds on this project: Molecular Modeling and Machine Learning Approaches for Neoantigen Prediction.

Cryptic allosteric pockets in full-length PTP1B

MD lead and mentor in a BSc graduation project, 2025–2026

I ran microsecond MD simulations of an AlphaFold3 model of full-length PTP1B to expose allosteric pockets around its C-terminal region, the starting point for a large virtual screening campaign on the national TRUBA HPC cluster.

Simulation at scale

I use GROMACS daily and ran a systematic GPU benchmark of it on TRUBA, about 600 runs across MPI, OpenMP and GPU settings (repository, write-up). I also wrote mdkit for reproducible analysis across many complexes and replicas.

What I want to work on

Learned structure predictors are fast but can be confidently wrong; simulations are interpretable but slow. I want to spend a PhD combining the two: generative and geometric models of conformational ensembles, checked and refined with MD, to find binding sites that matter for drug design and immunology.
